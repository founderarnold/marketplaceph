# FAQ open questions (owner to answer)

The FAQ and rider help only state facts that exist in the code. These are not defined anywhere yet, so they were left out instead of guessed. Answer them and they can be added to `lib/faq.ts`.

1. Rider fees: does MarketplacePH take any commission or fee from riders or from bookings? (Today riders set their own fee.)
2. Rider requirements: exact documents and age/vehicle rules for registration (the form asks for license, OR/CR, plate).
3. Rider disputes: what should a customer do if a rider damages or loses a parcel? Any response time?
4. Refunds and returns: is there a platform rule, or is it always between buyer and seller?
5. Dispute timelines: how long does a report take to review, and what is the response window for the other side? (The case workflow enforces a window; confirm the number of days to publish.)
6. Badges: the exact rules for Verified buyer, Credible employer and the tiers of seller verification.
7. Seller stops replying after payment: what is the recommended step, and when can a case be opened?
8. Fees: confirm that posting and selling have no commission, and that only the optional FLAME tiers cost money.
