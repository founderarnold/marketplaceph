# FAQ open questions

All eight questions were answered by the owner on 9 Nov 2026 and are now in `lib/faq.ts`:

1. Rider fees: none. Riders set their own fee.
2. Rider requirements: driver's license, OR/CR and plate number.
3. Damaged or lost parcel: customer settles it with the rider.
4. Refunds and returns: no platform rule. Always between buyer and seller (reminder shown at checkout and on each order).
5. Reports: reviewed within 72 hours; the other side has 72 hours to respond.
6. Badges: best-practice checks published in the FAQ ("How do badges get approved?"). Approval is still done by the team.
7. Seller stops replying: pay by COD or kaliwaan, avoid advance payments (reminder shown at checkout and on each order).
8. Fees: posting and selling are free, no commission. Only the optional FLAME plans cost money.
